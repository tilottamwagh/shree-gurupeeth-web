# Google Play listing assets

This folder contains the app listing artwork and authentic app UI captures.

## Ready-to-upload assets

- `play-icon-512.png` — 512 × 512 PNG with alpha; Google Play listing icon.
- `feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg` — 1024 × 500 JPEG; Google Play feature graphic.
- `phone/` — five 1080 × 1920 portrait PNG captures (home, daily worship, reading, calendar, mantra counter).
- `tablet/` — five 1920 × 1080 landscape PNG captures of the same areas.

The screenshots are unframed captures of the running app UI. The artwork sources are retained in `source-*.png` files for future edits.

## Before publishing

Upload the icon and feature graphic in the Play Console store listing. Upload at least four phone screenshots. Upload the tablet captures only if the app listing targets tablet devices; Play Console may request screenshots for additional supported device types. Keep screenshots representative of the released app and replace them whenever the UI changes.

These files meet the documented image dimensions and formats. Play Console can update its listing requirements, and asset validity does not replace completing the app's content rating, Data safety, target audience, privacy policy URL, app access, and other release declarations.
